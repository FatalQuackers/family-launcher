Things to try

  • Move with the ← ↑ → ↓ arrow keys or the remote. Enter is OK.
  • Move along Continue watching and Recently added and watch the Now playing banner change. Press OK to open it.
  • Hold OK on one of your apps to move or hide it. Hold OK on a show for Resume or Start over.
  • Quick actions has Google TV home, Sleep timer and Inputs.
  • The top bar shows who's watching what on Jellyfin elsewhere in the house, plus Search (or press /), the weather forecast and Settings.
  • Esc is Back and H is Home. The backgrounds move on their own; Settings › Wallpaper switches them.

The shows, people, weather and photos are made-up examples. On the TVs they'll come from your Jellyfin server, Open-Meteo and the photos folder. Your app layout and settings are saved in this browser.
